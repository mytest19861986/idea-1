WORKER INVARIANT — WORK-I028

FINAL_FAILURE and EXHAUSTED are different terminal semantics.

FINAL_FAILURE =
failure was non-retryable.

EXHAUSTED =
failure was retryable, but retry budget was exhausted.

maxAttempts must never cause a permanent/validation/policy failure
to be misrepresented as retry exhaustion.
